Privacy Policy for GREEBLIS TOWN

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how GREEBLIS TOWN handles user information.

GREEBLIS TOWN is a standalone virtual reality (VR) game available on the Meta Quest platform. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a gaming experience that does not require the collection or processing of personal information.

2. Information Collection

GREEBLIS TOWN does not collect, request, store, share, or transmit any personal information or user data.

The game does not require users to create an account, provide personal details, or submit any information to play. The game also does not save any data on the headset: no profile, progress, or settings are written.

3. Data Storage and Processing

GREEBLIS TOWN operates entirely within the user's VR headset.

The game includes a small local server that runs only inside the headset to load the game's own files. It accepts no connections from other devices and makes no connections to the internet. This is the only reason the application declares the Android network permission.

The game does not use external servers, cloud storage, or online databases to store or process user information.

No gameplay information, personal identifiers, or activity data is collected or transmitted by the game.

4. Third-Party Services

GREEBLIS TOWN does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

The game is distributed through the Meta Quest Store. Meta may independently collect and process information in accordance with its own privacy policies. Such data collection is outside the control of GREEBLIS TOWN.

5. Children's Privacy

GREEBLIS TOWN does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Because no personal information is collected from any user, no personal data is stored or shared by the game, regardless of the user's age.

6. Data Security

Since GREEBLIS TOWN does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal user data maintained by the game that could be accessed, disclosed, or compromised.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's functionality or privacy practices change.

Any updates will be reflected in the published Privacy Policy, along with a revised "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer of GREEBLIS TOWN at carmenjclipston@outlook.com.
