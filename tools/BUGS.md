## Possible fixes for the open bugs (not applied; the author decides)

Priority order requested by the author: B12 (white flashes) first.

**B12, white flashes on the environment (pickup while looking at the shop kiosk at a distance; garden ground during the full Event with Backpacka and music).** Candidates, each with a fix that needs no new content:
1. *Base-layer rebuild by the quality governor.* When the governor is already at its lowest level and frame time stays over 1.6x budget for 3 s it creates a new `XRWebGLLayer` at 0.82 scale and swaps it in; a layer swap renders one uncleared/partial frame on Quest. Fix: never rebuild the layer mid-session (keep one layer, scale 1.0, and let fixed foveation do the work; or create the 0.82 layer once at session start and switch render state only during a black fade). The Event is exactly when this trips.
2. *First-use GPU work during a pickup.* A pickup paints a new 64x64 canvas label texture, activates sparkle meshes, and may compile a sprite program the first time a label gets a map; one long frame makes the compositor show a re-projected frame that can read as a flash against a bright kiosk. Fix: pre-warm every label text and colour the game uses ("+1", "!", "✦", "♥", "✿", "~", "*", numbers) and the sparkle/splat materials at boot (`Ut.warm` already does 16 "✦" sprites; extend it), keep the textures alive (the 40-entry cache evicts).
3. *Batched shader part slots.* Batches update their per-part transform/colour uniform arrays (`gcR`) in `onBeforeRender`; a part re-indexed while a batch is rebuilt could be drawn one frame with a stale slot (wrong colour multiplier or transform). Fix: double-buffer the uniform array or only swap batches between frames. Detectable with the brightness probe.
4. *Comfort vignette quad.* A camera-attached full-view quad (`F.vignette`) with opacity driven by movement; if its texture were missing for a frame it would draw white. Low likelihood (it is created at session start with its texture), but the probe will show it.
5. *Lighting/tone changes.* Night materials' emissive intensity and the hemisphere/directional lights are updated per frame from `nightK`; a NaN or an un-clamped step would brighten everything for a frame. Fix: clamp and guard.
Plan: `tools/flash_probe.js` reads back each rendered frame's mean brightness during scripted pickups with the camera on the shop kiosk at a distance, and during the Event looking at the garden ground, and reports any frame brighter than its neighbours by a margin; then the cause above is confirmed or excluded one by one.

**B2, stuck NPCs teleport only when off-screen.** Fix options: (a) when a walker has been stuck longer than N seconds on-screen, fade it out over 0.3 s, move it, fade in (the entity groups already support opacity); (b) before teleporting, try the courier's detour logic (corner waypoints around the blocking part) once.

**B6, no in-VR loading indicator.** Fix options: (a) enter the XR session immediately on `sessiongranted` and show a small "Loading…" sprite in front of the camera while the world builds, which needs the boot to yield between steps (it is one synchronous call today); (b) shorten the boot instead by shipping pre-rendered textures and pre-merged geometry in the APK (size is no longer a limit), which also helps B12 candidate 2.

**B4 residual, random routes clipping a building corner by up to 0.3 m.** Fix options: (a) inflate the router's blocked-cell test by the walker radius plus 0.3 m; (b) post-process each route leg with `segBlocked(…, .3)` and insert the corner waypoint the courier detour uses. Cosmetic: the wall resolver pushes walkers out and B1 re-routes them.

**B9, headset launch.** 3.15.6 serves the asset-links file; if the next launch log still shows `TWA verification was unsuccessful`, compare the fingerprint in `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (fetch it with `adb shell curl http://localhost:17777/.well-known/assetlinks.json` while the app runs) with `apksigner verify --print-certs`, and check for `Digital Asset Links` lines in the log.
